Core Features

Physical Cash Meets Modern Cryptography

Physical Bearer Notes with Offline Verification

Printable SVG notes with tamper-evident seals carry redeemable Bitcoin value. Notes can be handed over in circular economies with zero fees and easily verifiable offline throught the app scanner or any other compatible scanner.

Multi-Mint Backing

BitNotes uses a hub-and-spoke mechanism to create multi-mint backed physical notes to reduce single mint reliance risks.

On-Chain Bitcoin Swaps

Deposit Bitcoin on-chain and have it automatically converted into private ecash tokens in the background or convert your ecash back to on-chain Bitcoin.

Personal Lightning Address

Get your own Lightning address to receive payments that automatically convert into private bearer ecash.

Mobile Money Bridge

Instantly convert Rwandan francs into Bitcoin satoshis and back via MoMo, making local payments and remittances easier.

Lifecycle

How Physical Ecash Works

01

Fund the wallet

Deposit satoshis into any trusted Cashu mint via ecash, Lightning, on-chain or Mobile Money.

02

Print Bearer Note

Generate and print out the physical note and conceal the private QR under a tamper-evident scratch seal.

03

Verify Offline & Sweep

Scan the public QR to verify a note offline, or peel the seal to redeem funds into a wallet or pay a Lightning invoice.
