Core Features

Tangible Cash Meets Modern Cryptography

Physical Bearer Notes with Offline Verification

Printable SVG notes with tamper-evident seals carry redeemable Bitcoin value. Issued via Cashu DHKE blind signatures, notes can be handed over in circular economies with zero fees and verified immediately offline using mint public keys before online redemption.

Multi-Mint Redundancy

Never rely on a single custodian. BitNotes pools and routes token proofs across independent mints (such as 28MINT) with automated hub-and-spoke swaps and NUT-15 multi-path payments.

Native Bitcoin & Ark

Onboard seamlessly from on-chain Bitcoin using Ark ASP boarding addresses, with automatic background sweeps into private ecash tokens.

Personal Lightning Address

Receive Lightning payments directly to your personal address (e.g., kenneth@28waves.com), automatically minted into private bearer ecash proofs via npub.cash.

Mobile Money Bridge

Instant conversion between Rwandan Francs (RWF via MoMo) and Bitcoin satoshis, creating an accessible bridge for local commerce and remittances.

Lifecycle

How Physical Ecash Works

01

Fund & Blind-Sign

Deposit satoshis into any trusted Cashu mint via Lightning or Mobile Money. The mint signs your blinded secrets without ever learning their values.

02

Print Bearer Note

Generate a high-density SVG note with a public QR for offline verification and a private QR concealed under a tamper-evident scratch seal.

03

Verify Offline & Sweep

Recipients verify note authenticity offline using the mint's public keys, or peel the seal to sweep funds directly into their wallet or Lightning invoice.
